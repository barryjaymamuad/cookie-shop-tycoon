# Kevin’s Cookie Shop Tycoon

Double-click **index.html** to play in any modern browser. Keep **game.js** in the same folder. No server, installation, or internet connection is required.

You start with $100 and have 30 days to run the shop. Each day, choose how many cookies to bake, their selling price, and your advertising spend. Cookies cost $0.50 to bake, and unsold cookies are discarded at closing time. Higher prices reduce demand, advertising boosts it, and customer traffic varies by day.

Select **Open shop** to watch customers come in and pick up cookies. Busier days show more shoppers, and each order adds to the live sales count. The receipt appears when the shop closes. If your system is set to reduce motion, the receipt appears immediately.

Expand **Sales history** to compare days. Your final result appears after Day 30. Reloading the page or starting a new game resets your progress.
